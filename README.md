## ✨ Overview

**Asset Management System** is a full-stack asset operations platform designed to give organizations a centralized view of their equipment, inventory, employees, assignments, maintenance workflows, and users.

The platform covers the complete asset lifecycle:

```text
Register
   ↓
Track
   ↓
Assign
   ↓
Maintain
   ↓
Repair
   ↓
Monitor
